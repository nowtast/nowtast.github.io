Rosary Privacy Policy · iOS

Scope

This policy applies to Rosary, provided by Chulsu Ha. Rosary works without an account and includes prayer texts and recordings on the device.

Information and how it is used

Rosary does not send registration details, location, device identifiers, or usage history to a developer server. It does not track usage for advertising or analytics. The app stores preferences such as language and playback speed, saved prayer progress, and draft correction reports on your device so you can keep your settings, resume a prayer, and edit a draft.

Retention and deletion

To erase all local app data, uninstall Rosary. Manage any copies you already shared in the receiving service.

On iOS, choose Delete App rather than Offload App.

Sharing and support

If you choose to share a correction report, the app can pass a copy to the destination you select. Review the report before sharing. The selected service’s privacy policy applies to its copy. If you email the developer for support, the developer uses your email address and the information you send to respond and resolve the issue.

Data protection

Rosary stores preferences, saved prayer progress, and correction drafts in the operating system’s private app storage. The app is configured to exclude this local data from operating system backups and does not automatically send it to a server.

Children’s privacy

The app does not automatically collect personal information from users, including children.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests to delete information you sent by email, contact Chulsu Ha (charlie.andrew.ha@gmail.com). If the app’s data practices change, we update the public privacy policy and the notice in the app.
